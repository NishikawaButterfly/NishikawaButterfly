# Hi, I'm NishikawaButterfly

**Electrical Engineer + Software Developer** focused on **data centers, energy systems, automation, and investment analytics**.

I build software that turns complex engineering workflows into practical, testable tools—from electrical asset validation and critical-infrastructure simulation to renewable-energy optimization and techno-economic analysis.

## Focus areas

- **Data centers and critical infrastructure** — electrical distribution, resilience, redundancy, controls, EPMS/SCADA concepts, and commissioning workflows.
- **Energy systems** — photovoltaic generation, battery energy storage systems, hybrid operation, dispatch optimization, and degradation-aware analysis.
- **Engineering automation** — structured-data validation, revision comparison, quality checks, traceable reporting, and workflow improvement.
- **Investment analytics** — NPV, IRR, payback, LCOE/LCOS, scenario analysis, sensitivity analysis, stress testing, and Monte Carlo methods.
- **Software product development** — APIs, interactive dashboards, relational data models, automated testing, and reproducible delivery.

## Selected projects

### Released

#### [Sudoku PWA](https://github.com/NishikawaButterfly/sudoku-pwa)

A responsive and installable Sudoku progressive web app with six difficulty levels, offline support, and automatic game saving.

`JavaScript` · `PWA` · `Responsive UI` · `Offline support`

#### [Electrical Asset Validator](https://github.com/NishikawaButterfly/electrical-asset-validator)

An engineering-data quality application for electrical asset registers. The released MVP validates CSV/XLSX data, detects duplicate and missing references, compares revisions, persists review history, and generates Excel/PDF reports using fully fictitious datasets.

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `PostgreSQL` · `Docker`

### Active modernization

#### [PV-BESS Hybrid Optimization](https://github.com/NishikawaButterfly/pv-bess-hibridacion)

A Python project for analyzing photovoltaic plants with battery storage. The current modernization phase is rebuilding the analytical prototype as an auditable engineering product with hourly dispatch, operational constraints, degradation, revenue analysis, and scenario-based investment metrics.

`Python` · `PV` · `BESS` · `Energy modeling` · `Optimization`

### Planned

#### Data Center Electrical Digital Twin

A simulation platform for data-center electrical architectures, including utility supplies, transformers, generators, UPS, ATS/STS, PDUs, IT loads, redundancy configurations, failure scenarios, event timelines, and simulated Modbus/SCADA data.

#### Energy Asset Investment Lab

A technical investment-analysis platform for renewable generation, BESS, pumped-storage hydro, hybrid assets, and data-center infrastructure, covering cash flows, financing, NPV, IRR, LCOE/LCOS, sensitivities, stress tests, and Monte Carlo analysis.

An evidence-led portfolio site that presents released engineering products, verified case studies, technical decisions, demonstrations, and a downloadable résumé without duplicating each repository as the source of truth.
